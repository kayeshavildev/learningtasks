Caesar Royale Salad

Crisp, hand-selected romaine hearts gently tossed in a velvety Caesar emulsion, layered with golden garlic croutons and a snowfall of aged Parmesan. Finished with a bright citrus lift and an optional crown of flame-grilled chicken for added richness and depth.

Calories & Nutrition

  • Calories: 350–650 kcal (with or without chicken)
  • Serving Size: 1 bowl (250g–350g)
  • Protein: 10–35g
  • Carbohydrates: 15–35g
  • Fat: 20–45g
  • Allergens: Contains gluten, dairy, and egg (anchovy optional in dressing)
  • Main Ingredients: Romaine lettuce, Caesar dressing, croutons, Parmesan cheese, lemon

Ingredients

  • Romaine lettuce – Fresh, crisp leaves
  • Caesar dressing – Creamy emulsion with anchovy (optional)
  • Croutons – Golden, crispy bread cubes
  • Parmesan cheese – Aged, grated cheese
  • Lemon – Freshly squeezed juice

Allergen Notice: Please inform us of any food allergies before ordering.

Kitchen Notice: Prepared in a kitchen that handles gluten, dairy, and eggs.
