Creamy Carbonara Pasta

Silky spaghetti tossed in a rich, velvety carbonara sauce made with egg yolk, aged cheese, and cracked black pepper. Finished with smoky bacon and a light snowfall of Parmesan for a deeply savory, comforting classic.

  • Bacon – Smoky, crispy strips for texture
