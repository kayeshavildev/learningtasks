House Special Pizza Classic

A hand-stretched pizza base baked to golden perfection, layered with creamy Caesar emulsion, tender romaine-inspired toppings, and a rich snowfall of aged Parmesan. Finished with crispy garlic crouton-style crunch and a bright citrus touch, with an optional topping of flame-grilled chicken for added depth and flavor.
